One settlement pivot

Users keep their assets.
Providers use M1.

Burning BTC creates M0. Locking M0 creates M1: working capital for providers, connecting quoted pairs through one inventory.

The burn is irreversible. Unlocking M1 returns M0, not BTC. No redeemable BTC reserve, no guaranteed peg or liquidity.
