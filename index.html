Bitcoin facts. Shared rules.

Settlement rooted in Bitcoin.

Each node verifies Bitcoin facts. M0 is the settlement asset, created only by burning bitcoin. N selects one producer per slot from burned tickets, without a vote.

N-SPEC v0.7, preamble; §8.4; §4.9; §7.1; APP-SPEC v1 draft §0.4 (in French).

What BATHRON does

Bitcoin facts without an oracle.

Each node checks Bitcoin evidence against the reference designated by the N block. BATHRON reads Bitcoin without changing its rules.

N-SPEC v0.7, preamble, point 3; §8.4; §11.1.

Explore the N engine

One settlement asset, no issuer.

M0 comes from burned bitcoin. Fees transfer existing M0; production creates no block reward. A burn creates no reserve and no claim on Bitcoin.

APP-SPEC v1 draft §0.4; N-SPEC v0.7 §1.4, invariants 2 and 12; §4.9; WHY-N §3.

Understand burns

Wait or stop rather than be wrong.

Participants choose depth within the published domain. Settlement may wait hours, and conflicts may halt progress. Outside that domain, N makes no quantitative claim; STOP need not occur.

WHY-N §7; §8; N-SPEC v0.7 §14.4; §17.6; §18.
