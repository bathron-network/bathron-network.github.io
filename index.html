The problem

Today a market exists because an exchange lists it — and dies when the exchange delists it.

Two parties who both hold inventory still cannot settle against each other without handing custody to a third.

Bitcoin settles value. It deliberately refuses to express the conditions of a trade.

Today

asset → listing committee → market

Born by permission. Dies by decision.

On BATHRON

inventory + quotes → market

Born when two parties can settle. Nobody can revoke what nobody granted.

What BATHRON provides

Three things. Only three.

A common settlement unit — M1

Every settlement on the network is expressed in one numéraire. M1 is created only from verified, irreversible Bitcoin destruction. Nobody issues it; nobody can print it. Market builders and providers hold it as working capital — end users keep settling in the assets they already hold.

A consensus that guarantees settlement

Accounting integrity, Bitcoin facts verified inside consensus, the conditions written into contracts, and transfers — final in about a minute. Every node checks every block; a finality quorum can order, never mint.

An infrastructure any market can be built on

Covenants, timelocks, hashlocks, Bitcoin-fact verification and confidential transfers — composable by anyone, enforced by every node, frozen so that what grows is what is built on top.

What BATHRON deliberately does not do

No order book·No matching engine·No listing·No published price·No chosen market maker·No token sale·No premine·No treasury·No yield

The consensus only settles. Everything else is yours.

Bitcoin does not decide who may hold BTC. BATHRON does not decide which markets may exist.

How a market appears

Nobody approves any of it.

1

Bring inventory

The asset to pair, and M1.

2

Publish quotes

Signed, off-chain, on any relay. The consensus never sees them.

3

Settle

A counterparty accepts. The M1 leg settles on BATHRON; an external-asset leg settles on its native chain under linked conditions.

4

Others join

Another provider quotes the same pair, tighter or deeper. No invitation needed.

BTC/M1·PIVX/M1·DOGE/M1·LTC/M1·any/M1

Native BTC ⇄ M1 is paired with hashlocked legs on both chains sharing one preimage — the components are demonstrated on testnet; a generally atomic client flow is not yet claimed. Any chain that supports hashlocks and timelocks can be paired the same way: a capability of the primitives, not a shipped product.

Why Bitcoin stays at the center

Bitcoin sees two transactions. BATHRON settles everything in between.

Bitcoin is the final asset — the reserve of value and the only origin of M1: BTC → M0 → M1, one way, verified by SPV inside consensus.

BATHRON never replaces Bitcoin and never holds it. There is no reserve and no redemption — what brings native BTC back is a market, not a vault.

Bitcoin facts are checked by every node. No oracle, no federation, no designated attester.

Who is who

Operators

Run consensus: produce blocks, sign finality, publish facts about themselves. One operator, one vote. They never rank, never choose a provider, never set a price.

Settlement Providers

Participants, not administrators. A Clearing Provider quotes and orchestrates; a Liquidity Provider holds inventory and prices a pair. They appear and disappear freely.

You

Build a market, an application, or simply settle. The protocol asks nothing of you but a node.

Where we are

Experimental. Public testnet live. No mainnet.

Anyone can build, quote, pair and settle without asking permission. Operator admission is not yet open — the current operator set is project-run while the open-admission threat model is worked. No external audit yet. No proven market.
