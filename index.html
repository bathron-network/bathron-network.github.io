Facts, not opinions

Bitcoin already proves things. Now a contract can use them.

Every Bitcoin block carries facts nobody can forge. A BATHRON contract can depend on those facts directly, and every node on the network checks them for itself.

Mining difficulty

How hard the network was working at a given point in Bitcoin's history.

Block height

How far the chain has advanced.

Median time

Bitcoin's own clock, resistant to a single miner's timestamp.

Confirmed payments

That a Bitcoin transaction paid a given script, proved and settled.

Programmable settlement

Write the conditions. The network enforces them.

Settlement on BATHRON is described in script and carried out by consensus. The same primitives compose, so a single agreement can depend on a Bitcoin fact, a secret and a deadline at once.

Covenants

Constrain what a spend is allowed to become.

Hashlocks

Release value against the reveal of a secret.

Timelocks

Make an outcome depend on time, absolute or relative.

Signatures and attestations

Verify a signature over any message, including one from an outside source.

Confidential transfers

Move value without publishing the amount.

A network of distinct roles

Three roles, kept apart on purpose.

Consensus Operators

Secure the network, order transactions and finalize them. One operator, one vote.

Settlement Providers

Create the services and settlement flows that people actually use. They compete, and they come and go.

Builders

Compose applications and instruments on top, choosing their own interfaces and their own terms.

No consensus operator chooses which application may be built.

Bitcoin remains the anchor

The anchor is Bitcoin, and it stays where it is.

Bitcoin facts are verified by the nodes themselves. M0 originates from Bitcoin destruction confirmed by consensus, and M1 is the settlement unit that follows from the M0 and M1 rules.

BATHRON holds no bitcoin. It offers no reserve, no parity and no redemption. Bitcoin comes back to a holder through a market, the way any other asset does.

Markets, as one application

A pair exists because someone quotes it.

A provider holding inventory can publish offers and build a market. There is no listing committee to convince and no registry to enter, and a second provider can quote the same pair whenever it wants to.
